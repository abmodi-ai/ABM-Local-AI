## Reviewer notes (spot-check of the grader, 2026-09-28)

- **The grader is mostly right** about made-up claims. Typical flags are added conclusions not in
  the sources, e.g. "…treated with the same integrity as paper records".
- **It has some false positives, in both directions of strictness:**
  - It flagged a correct Part 11 audit-trail sentence as unsupported.
  - It flagged "2003" because it sees passage text but not document titles.

  Both models' made-up rates are therefore somewhat overstated. The ranking doesn't change.
- **The refusal counts are strict** (the refusal sentence and nothing else). Counting answers
  that *open* with the refusal sentence and then explain why, Qwen3.5 refused 14/16 unanswerable
  prompts and Granite 10/16.
- **Fix before the expert run:** show the grader each passage's document title as well as its text.
